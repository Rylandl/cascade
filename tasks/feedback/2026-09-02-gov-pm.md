# Review: government autonomy program manager (Opus role-play, 2026-09-02)

Reviewed at v0.2.0 (9f2fa9c).

## 1. Does this support the programme thesis today?

Partly: as infrastructure, not as evidence. Demonstrated: a differentiable batched core with coefficient and panel backends; archetypes that sample flyable airframes and a tuner that produces a working cascade with no human in the loop (about 80% of sampled designs settle); families stacked for one vmap; Dryden weather in reset/step; MuJoCo playback; CI on two OSes; MIT; py.typed.

Slide-worthy: auto-tuning generalises to about 80% of randomly sampled designs with no hand tuning; about 9 in 10 sampled designs pass trim and authority validation; 75k to 130k env steps/s at batch 1024 on a laptop CPU.

Not slide-worthy:
- "Validated against real flight." The X8 anchor is replay prediction on one campaign, and the 0.677-of-persistence score is the best cell of a CG x mass x inertia x vertical-wind grid (2x the measured pendulum inertia, half the inferred vertical wind). Docs disagree: README 0.68/1.33x, todo 0.735/1.44x, skywalker-x8.md 0.677.
- "Learned control matches the tuned cascade." 156.8 vs 156.5 of 160 is one seed, no variance, a fictional fixture, small perturbations, trim-initialised. The honest counter-result (transition learning fails, 28 vs 142) is more informative.
- "GPU scaling." No GPU measurement exists.

The thesis metric, time-to-competence on a new airframe, is not implemented (todo 7e).

## 2. What a credible demonstration needs

| Element | Missing today |
| --- | --- |
| Metric: simulated (and real) flight-minutes to reach 90% of the per-airframe auto-tuned score | Not defined or coded |
| Baselines: auto-tuned cascade, trim-hold, and a non-adaptive policy trained on the family | Only the cascade exists |
| Held-out protocol: frozen hashed manifest of N designs, train/test split | sample_family draws fresh designs per key; no manifest, split, or seed discipline |
| Weather protocol: named conditions, held-out station records, spatially correlated gusts | Dryden at a point; no station data ships; no 1-cos gusts, ISA density, shear, thermals |
| Real-flight anchor: closed-loop flight or HIL with Cascade-derived gains on 2+ airframes | None; replay only, one airframe; no PX4/MAVLink/HIL path |
| Independent reproduction: pinned release, one command per doc table | Git-install only; no PyPI/DOI; scripts exist for figures and fits but not throughput, learning, or weather numbers |

## 3. Unserved needs, ranked

1. Adaptation benchmark with held-out airframes: frozen manifest, splits, seeds, leaderboard script, 5+ seeds with variance. 2 to 3 months.
2. Closed-loop real-flight anchor: sim-tuned gains flown on X8-class hardware. 4 to 6 months, needs flight assets.
3. Takeoff/landing and ground contact. About 2 months for penalty contact.
4. Known-wrong dynamics that hit control design: panel X8 pitch damping 2x measured, no downwash, static slipstream. About 3 months.
5. Richer weather and density altitude. About 1 month.
6. GPU/scale evidence and a Gymnasium shim. About 2 weeks.

## 4. Risks

Technical: quasi-steady aero with one hysteresis state where the thesis lives (post-stall, transition); the 2x pitch-damping error is a bandwidth-setting error. Evidence: variant-grid fitting on the evaluation set; single-seed learning claims; doc-to-doc numeric inconsistency; archetypes plausible not validated. Transition: no flight stack, HIL, or airworthiness artefacts; the real-flight chain runs through Glassbox whose licence is undecided. Sustainment: bus factor 1, alpha, no external contributors.

## 5. What I would fund first

WP-1, 6 months, "Time-to-Competence Harness": freeze a 100-design two-archetype benchmark (manifest, hashes, MIT station-record weather set); implement the flight-minutes-to-baseline metric; ship the three baselines; a reproduction container and one command per doc table; re-run the X8 anchor with the variant grid fixed a priori.

Go/no-go: on 20 held-out designs, one adaptation procedure reaches 90% of the per-airframe auto-tuned score within 10 simulated flight-minutes in moderate turbulence over 5+ seeds with variance, reproduced independently within 10%; and the pre-registered X8 replay score stays below 1.0 of persistence without grid selection.
