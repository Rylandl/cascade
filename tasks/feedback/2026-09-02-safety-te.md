# Review: flight-test and airworthiness lead (Opus role-play, 2026-09-02)

## 1. Could this shorten a qualification campaign, and where

Yes, but only as a test-planning and build-up tool, not as credit-for-test. Highest-value uses: gain qualification before first flight (autotune + step_response removes two to four gain-hunting sorties per axis); test-point selection for VTOL transition (the corridor's coexisting branches above 7 m/s and the 3 to 5 m/s authority pinch should set build-up order, airspeed increments, and the abort trigger); candidate wind limits and the hover-azimuth procedure as a hypothesis to spot-check; dispersion screening via per-world parameters and families at 75 to 130k steps/s. No credit for takeoff, landing, ground handling, failure cases, or structural limits.

## 2. V&V of the simulation itself

Verified, genuinely strong: torque-free tumble conserves momentum and energy to 1e-5 over 10 s; finiteness and finite gradients at zero airspeed and through the full atan2 range; the X8 body block reproduces Gryte et al. Table V to 2e-5; Dryden variances match MIL-F-8785C; batched and single evaluation agree; frame conventions pinned by test.

Validated, thin and honestly reported: one airframe, one campaign, a rolling-window score of 0.677 of persistence, and only at a variant with CG moved 50 mm, inertia doubled, vertical wind halved. Nothing in the stall and post-stall regime has been compared to flight. Tailsitter and archetypes are fixtures.

Traceability defect: the same result appears as 0.68/1.33x (README), 0.677/0.675 (skywalker-x8.md), and 0.735/1.44x (todo) under three variants. A board would demand: a per-regime validation report with tolerance-banded overlays rather than a scalar; configuration control stamping model hash, spec version, JAX/hardware, seed and dtype on every result (no x64 policy stated); numerical regression baselines in CI; a statement of intended use and limits; a defect log.

## 3. Hazard and envelope coverage gaps, ranked

1. Ground contact, takeoff, landing: absent by design; where most mishaps live.
2. Failure injection: no fault model (jam, hardover, motor-out, partial power); expressible by hand, not by API.
3. Departure and spin: no autorotation; the panel X8 gets C_np and C_Yp signs wrong, the terms that govern departure.
4. Dynamic stall: quasi-steady with a first-order lag; no convective time scaling; stall entry rate is the qualification question.
5. Transition failure cases: corridor is steady state; no thrust asymmetry, no failed back-transition energy case.
6. Atmosphere: no discrete 1-cosine gust (the certification-standard case), no shear/rotor/thermal, no vertical mean wind, no density altitude.
7. Estimator/link failures: GNSS loss, link loss, failsafe, geofence absent.

## 5. The one artefact

An Envelope Clearance Card per airframe build and proposed test point: the dispersed outcome distribution over a declared uncertainty set, the margin to each limit (alpha to stall, channel saturation, load factor, altitude floor), the predicted recovery, and a validation-pedigree block stating which quantities have been compared against flight, in what regime, to what tolerance. One command, one signed page.
